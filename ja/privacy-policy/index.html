Privacy Policy

WE CARE ABOUT YOUR PRIVACY

We limit the information collected about you to what is needed for conducting business and offering products and services that might be of interest to you. You may choose to provide individually identifiable information (such as name, telephone number, and email address) to iSunOne through our mobile APP. Our website is merely a landing page for the APP and does not store any personal information.

​

 

WE DO NOT:

1. Collect individually identifiable information from you unless you provide it to us.

2. Sell the individually identifiable information of APP end users and website visitors to any third parties.

​

 

WE DO:

Use security techniques designed to protect customers’ information from unauthorized access. Collect, maintain and use customer information as necessary to provide the most helpful and targeted products and services to meet customers’ needs. Honor customers’ requests to remove their names from our email solicitation lists using reasonable efforts to delete the information from existing files.

​

 

COOKIES

A cookie is a small, encrypted data string our server writes to you to deliver web content specific to your interests after your initial entry. With most browsers, you can choose to change your browser settings to disable cookies.

 

DATA SECURITY

We have security measures in place to protect against the loss, misuse and alteration of information under our control. When you place an order, the information you submit is encrypted using the Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) protocol. Your personal information, including Visa credit card information, is contained behind a Payment Card Industry (PCI) compliant server, and is only accessible by a limited number of operational persons who are required to keep the information confidential. The IP access to the server uses anti-DDOS and whitelisting techniques to conform to data security. We implement a variety of security measures when a user enters, submits, or accesses their information to maintain the safety of your personal information.

 

​

LINKS

iSunOne is not responsible for content or privacy policies of other sites. You should keep this in mind when accessing sites through links on iSunOne site.

​

 

EMAIL

If you need to unsubscribe from our emails, you can do so by clicking on the unsubscribe link within your email, or by emailing hello@iSun1.com.

UPDATES

As with all iSunOne sites and mobile APP, we will constantly strive to improve the services we may offer, and may update this privacy statement from time to time in connection with that effort. We reserve the right to expand our capabilities for information collection and use, and may change our policies along with this privacy statement at any time.
