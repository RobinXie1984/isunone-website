Risk Disclaimer

The iSunOne Global Visa Card is a prepaid card solution provided in strict adherence and partnership with regulated financial institutions, including but not limited to  the Visa card network. Upon funding the prepaid card, the custody of fiat funds is transferred to the Visa card network, thereby subject to their respective rules and regulations.  To ensure the security of your funds, it is imperative that you safeguard your card diligently. Employ your password, 2-factor-authentication, and biometric authorization to access your card, and abstain from disclosing your card information to any unauthorized third parties.  Never store your card information on any unauthorized browser or website.  In the unfortunate event of your card being misplaced or stolen, we strongly advise that you promptly inform iSunOne.  It is important to note that iSunOne cannot be held accountable for any losses or theft of funds occurring on the prepaid card.  However, in the event of such unfortunate circumstances, iSunOne is committed to swiftly assisting you in freezing the card to mitigate any potential damages.

As a US-based compliant corporation, iSunOne is regulated federally as a money service business (MSB) by FinCEN, and complies with local State regulators.
