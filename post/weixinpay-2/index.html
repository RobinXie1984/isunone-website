Wechat Pay & iSunOne Global Visa Card: The Perfect Fusion

iSun One
Aug 30, 2023
2 min read

iSunOne Global Visa Card is a prepaid card that offers unlimited recharges, with a single charge limit of up to $25,000. You can charge your iSunOne card with digital dollars like USDT and USDC, allowing you to enjoy convenient consumption and borderless payments.

Get my Card

The iSunOne Global Visa Card can be seamlessly linked to your WeChat Pay, enabling its use for various online and offline consumption and payment scenarios, covering all aspects of your daily life.


The iSunOne team has provided a guide on how to link your iSunOne Global Visa Card with WeChat Pay as follows:


1. Switch your wallet region to Mainland China;


2. Tap on the wallet and select "Credit Card/Debit Card,"

then follow the prompts to input relevant information;


3. Agree to the terms and conditions to successfully add your VISA card or Mastercard;



4. To pay with Wechat Pay, open the payment QR code and set the card payment as the preferred method.


Please note, as the iSunOne Global Visa Card is a USD-based international prepaid card, once linked successfully with WeChat Pay, transactions below CNY 200 will not incur transaction fees. Transactions exceeding CNY 200 will be subject to a 3% fee by WeChat Pay.


-WeChat Pays international card usage limits are as follows:

  • Single transaction limit: CNY 6,000

  • Monthly transaction limit: CNY 50,000

  • Annual transaction limit: CNY 60,000

WeChat Pay only provides domestic daily consumption services within mainland China, excluding overseas spending, financial management, or transfers.


-iSunOne Global Visa Card usage limits:

  • There are no consumption limits; you can spend as much as the balance on your card.

  • No monthly fees, annual fees, payment fees, with unlimited charges.

  • Usable globally at any merchant or platform accepting Visa.

We believe that digital currency payments will undoubtedly shape the future, and iSunOne is committed to providing users with top-notch global payment services and future virtual banking account services.
